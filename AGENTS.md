# AGENTS.md

How ROG Deck is put together, for anyone (or any agent) changing it. The user-facing story is in [README.md](README.md).

## Layout

- `Service.qml`: always loaded. Owns the status poller, the action queue to `bin/rog-deck`, `~/.config/rog-deck/settings.json`, the background jobs (keyboard auto-off, Fn lock after login, Auto refresh rate, theme re-apply) and the IPC target.
- `Panel.qml`: the bar entry, its hover card and the panel, once per monitor, drawing the service's state. Bar picks come from `WidgetSettings.qml`. It holds what the rooms read (`host` in every room file): the sizes (`roomPad`, `roomGap`, `iconColumn`, `textInset`, `panelWidth`), `toneOf(room)`, `lowTone`, the one clock (`entry`, `clock`, `wake`), `openRoom`/`toggleRoom`, `view`/`setView`, and the prompt (`heroLine`).
- `components/RoomsPage.qml`: the main page. The title in a neutral room (`HeroBlock.qml`), the banners, `PowerRoom.qml` the whole width, then `HeatRoom` (the CPU and GPU) across most of a row with `FansRoom` and `BatteryRoom` stacked beside it as two tiles that make a square, then two rooms to a row: `GraphicsRoom`/`DisplayRoom`, `KeyboardRoom`/`AnimeRoom`. A room the laptop lacks leaves its neighbour the space. A row's open room drawer is `PairDrawer.qml`, a room the width of the row in the open room's tone, holding that room's `*Drawer.qml`; the power room's drawer is inside it (`Drawer.qml`). `HeatRoom` has no header of its own: each part is a `RoomHeader` (icon, name, warmth, temperature on the right) over a full-width meter, so neither half has empty space beside its figure. A tile (`TileHeader.qml`) is its icon and its figure, a size under a room's (`font.caption` − 2, padding 7), centred together as one group, no name: the room's `tip` names it and says its state on hover. The two tiles are as tall as the CPU and GPU room, and the stack as wide as that, or wider where a tile's widest figure (100%) needs it. Two half rooms in a row share a header height (`headerNatural`, `headerMin`), so their faders line up. `SettingsPage.qml` (the gear) and `FansPage.qml` (the curve editor, `FanCurves.qml` and `FanCurve.qml`) are the other two pages.
- The Rooms pieces, the same as Sonus's: `Room.qml` (tint, rise, ripple, click), `RoomHeader.qml` (pixel icon, name and chevron, a decoding state line, `BigFigure.qml`), `Meter.qml` (cells; with `interactive` it is a fader that commits on release), `Decode.qml`, `Drawer.qml`, `HeroBlock.qml`, `PixelIcon.qml`, `PixelText.qml`, `PixelButton.qml`, `HoverCard.qml` and `HoverRow.qml`. ROG Deck's own: `ChoiceRow.qml` (a pick of one as small cells: modes, GPU modes, rates, timeouts, presets), `LightFader.qml` (a light's on and off button in the icon column, then a cell per level, as Sonus lays out a fader), `LevelPick.qml` (a labelled fader for a limit), `Facts.qml` (readings two to a line), `RoomPicks.qml` (a room's own bar switches).
- `assets/fonts/`: Monocraft 4.2.1 (SIL OFL 1.1, `OFL.txt` beside it), built by `scripts/patch-font.py` (shared with the other three plugins) as the family "Monocraft ROG Deck": ten font pixels to the em, an 11-pixel line (9 over the baseline, 2 under), a few half-pixel glyphs nudged onto the grid, and ❯, ▌ and a zero-width space added. Each plugin's copy has a name of its own because all four load into one shell, and Qt drew whichever registered first. Run the script on any new copy of the font; `tests/test_kit.py` checks the result. `Panel.qml` loads the regular face with `FontLoader` (the bold is never drawn) and hands the family out as `fontFamily`, which every text item reads through `host`; `gridFamily`, the font `BigFigure` lays its block cells on, is the same family.
- `components/Rooms.js`: the look's pure logic (tones, block face, pixel type, meter cells, faders, stories, the fan's spin, decode, the prompt's reveal, the glyph map), tested by `tests/rooms.test.js`.
- `components/Pixels.js`: every icon, pixel art from Pixelarticons (MIT), built by `scripts/fetch-pixel-icons.py` (source SVGs in `pixels/`). The script also draws what the set lacks on the same grid (the fan, its blades' turned frame, the AniMe Matrix) the dials for Balanced and Turbo (Pixelarticons' speed icons are half circles, half as tall as the bar's other icons), and a CPU (Pixelarticons' own reads as a sun at twelve pixels). Its `PARTS` splits an icon into a `base` that never moves and `parts` that do, each with its frames: some of the icon's own cells, picked on the 12-cell grid, or cells drawn there (the needle's three positions, the mercury's levels, the Z over the bed). Add a name to its `ICONS` and run it; `--show` prints the grids, `--parts` each icon's parts lettered and every frame.
- `components/Motion.js`: what each icon acts out (`STORIES`, one per icon, tested in `tests/rooms.test.js`) and the loops. `PixelIcon.qml` plays it: `play()` once, `loop` while something is live, `playOnShow` as its page appears.
- `Model.js`: parsing and formatting, and what each room says, tested by `tests/model.test.js`.
- `bin/rog-deck`: the backend (Python): asusd over D-Bus, power-profiles-daemon, sysfs, hyprctl, `nvidia-smi` only while the dGPU is awake. `rog-deck update` hands over to `bin/plugin-update`.
- `bin/rog-deck-power`: the root helper for Silent's CPU limit, panel power savings, Wi-Fi power save and full speed's CPU energy preference. `rog-deck power setup` copies it to `/usr/local/libexec/rog-deck-power` with a polkit rule for the user; `bin/rog-deck` loads this file only to read its `LIMITS` table.
- `docs/PLAN.md`: the original design notes.

## Panel kit

`SectionHeader`, `StatTile`, `ToggleTile`, `SwitchRow`, `Seg`, `Notice`, `LevelBar`, `OptionRow`, `Caption` and `UpdateSection` in `components/` are the panel kit: the same files, byte for byte, in ROG Deck, Batteries, Sonus and Server Dashboard, so the four look like one family. Change a kit file in all four or not at all. `Glyph.qml` is the one part each plugin owns: here it looks the Nerd Font glyph up in `Rooms.PIXEL_GLYPHS` and draws its pixel icon, so the kit's icons match the rooms'. Map every glyph ROG Deck hands the kit there; `tests/rooms.test.js` fails on one that is missing. The one icon it cannot reach is the "Check now" button inside `UpdateSection`. A `Seg` types its icons, so give it none; a pick with icons is a `ChoiceRow`.

`Updater.qml`, `Update.js` and `bin/plugin-update` are the shared updater (ROG Deck, Batteries and Server Dashboard; Sonus's engine updates itself the same way). It stashes local edits, untracked files too, before fast-forwarding, and drops the stash when the edits already are the update. `tests/update.test.js` and `tests/test_plugin_update.py` cover it.

Panels never cut text: a `Notice` shows all of its message, a `Caption` breaks long words and paths, and a `ToggleTile` label that does not fit shows in full in its tooltip.

## Panel look

The panel is in the Rooms look Batteries 2.8 set and Sonus 1.8 carried (the brief is `files get key:roomstheme`): a room per part of the laptop, tinted in its own tone, numbers in a block face, pixel icons, motion that answers what happens. The settings page keeps the panel kit, so it matches the other plugins. Scaled through `Style`; the panel is `Style.space(520)` so two rooms share a row with their figures whole (about 478 on this machine).

- Rooms: padding 10, joined by 3, `Style.cornerRadius`, the tone at 0.065 alpha, 0.11 under the pointer or while open. Cells inside a room (`ChoiceRow`) at 0.075, 0.2 and outlined for the one in use.
- Tones (`Model.ROOM_TONES`, `Rooms.roomToneHex`): power blue, CPU and GPU cyan, fans magenta (cyan beside them, blue above), battery green (under the fans), graphics green, display yellow, keyboard cyan, AniMe magenta, the mark (the app icon's flame) yellow; the title room none. Neighbours never share one, beside or above (`tests/model.test.js` checks the grid in quarters). The theme's colour of that name from its `colors.toml` (read on each open) when it is a colour, else DailyDash's hue, softened 30% toward the text colour. Hot and low use `lowTone`, the theme's urgent colour where it reads on the background, and always come with a mark (`thermometer`, `battery-low`). Only the panel and the hover card take tones; the bar keeps the bar's colours, urgent only for 85 °C and up.
- A room's header: pixel icon at `font.heading` × 1.25 in the tone, `font.body` bold name with a `font.caption` chevron that flips when open, a `font.caption` state line dimmed, the figure as `BigFigure` on the `font.bodySmall` grid with its unit (`W`, `%`, `Hz`, `°`) in the pixel type and a mark over it (`zap` while full speed holds or the battery charges, `plug` on the charger holding, `reload` for a GPU mode waiting on a restart). A state line stays short enough not to wrap past two lines in half a row: the figure carries the number.
- Meters: cells across the room (ten for a level or a temperature, three for a light: Low, Med, High), track at 0.12, outline at 0.6, no nub. A limit is a 1 px tick. A light's meter picks by cell (`perCell`, `Rooms.cellAt`): a press anywhere in a cell is that level, never the nearest step to the pointer. Off is the button before it, which keeps the level dimmed. Limits (charge, CPU watts) snap to their step. Faders write once, on release.
- The bar: the mode's icon (`leaf`, then the dials `speed-medium` and `speed-fast`, drawn as three-quarter dials so they fill the square like the shell's own icons) at the largest sharp size under the shell's icons, picked readings after it as pixel icon plus pixel type, 5 between items and 3 between an icon and its figure. Only the mode's name is a word, in Monocraft like the panel's text. The hover card is a row per reading (`Model.hoverRows`).

Motion, all on one clock (`entry` goes up on each open; `clock` ticks only while a ripple runs) and all off with reduced motion:

- Opening: rooms rise 12 and fade in, 70 ms apart; meters fill from empty over 680 ms while their figures count up; state lines and readings decode; each icon acts out its thing (`Motion.STORIES`, below) as its room lands; the settings page's icons play top to bottom as it appears, and the hover card's down the card; the prompt types facts (`Model.heroPhrases`) a new one every 2.8 s. A GPU switch waiting on a restart or a missing asusd holds the prompt still; an open room makes it say what the room holds (`Model.roomLine`).
- While open, only what is live loops, off one shared clock (`Motion.LOOP_MS`, the wall clock folded; every loop's period divides it): the fan's blades turn while the fans spin, faster for faster fans (`Motion.spinInterval`), and stop when they stop; a charging battery's bars fill every 4.8 s; a hot reading's thermometer climbs every 2.4 s; a GPU switch waiting on a restart runs its arrow round every 4.8 s. Nothing else moves on its own.
- Icons: each acts out what it is, in whole cells of its 12-cell grid, and only the part that moves moves: the dial's needle drops back and swings to the mode, the leaf drifts, the fan's blades turn round a hub that stays, the CPU's pins pulse while its die blinks, the GPU's fans whirr, the mercury climbs, a line sweeps down the screen, the keys light up from the left, the matrix's LEDs come on corner to corner, the battery's bars fill, the flame flickers, the sun's rays flash in turn, the light's off slash draws across. Every story starts and ends at rest (the test checks it), so nothing jumps when one ends. Triggers: the room landing on open, a click, the pointer arriving, and the state the icon stands for changing while the panel is open (`RoomHeader.cue`: the mode, the warmth word, the rate, the level, the GPU mode, plugging in). A play already running is never restarted.
- A refresh never replays the opening. Every figure goes through `Counter.qml` (counts up on opening, then slides 420 ms to each new reading) and every meter through `Meter.slide`. An animation's `to` is set as it starts, never bound: bound, it still held the old value when the change handler ran, and the slide went nowhere. Repeaters fed data that refreshes are kept alive: `HeatRoom` builds its cells from `partKeys`, which changes only when the parts do; `ChoiceRow` rebuilds only when its options' JSON changes (and wraps into rows of equal count when its widest name won't fit a cell); `Facts` and the hover card repeat by count and read their rows live. A rebuilt `Meter` would fill again from empty. The prompt types a line out only when `Panel.heroKey` (which line it is, apart from its numbers) changes; a new reading in the same line just swaps.
- Pointer: cells under it give; a click ripples through the room's meter, plays the icon again and opens the room, its drawer sliding over 280 ms. A new power mode swings the needle, in the room, on its cell and in the bar. Pages slide 18 px in from their side and the gear turns in 45° steps.

Check layout at the panel's real width with long values and three-digit figures: `Style.space` scales. The offscreen audit (`files get key:roomstheme`, "Auditing offscreen") renders `RoomsPage`, `SettingsPage` and `FansPage` with a stand-in host.

## Rules that are easy to break

- **Settings** are read with `setting(key, fallback)` and saved with `persistSettings({...})`. Both go through `WidgetSettings.qml`, which reads `~/.config/omarchy/shell.json` itself. Never read `settings.foo` directly: the bar's copy turns a list into a Qt sequence that fails `Array.isArray`, and after a plugin reload it can be older than the file. Either one makes a pick look unset, which is how choices "reset after a reboot" or differ between monitors.
- **One widget per monitor.** The bar draws the widget on every screen. Long-running work (a poller, a client, a process) lives in one place, a leader instance or the service, and the others read from it. Never start it per instance.
- **IPC** is answered by whichever monitor's widget registered first, so `open`, `close` and `toggle` go through the shell's `summon`, `hide` and `toggle`, which pick the focused monitor.
- **Nothing may write inside the plugin folder at runtime.** The shell watches it and reloads every plugin when a file appears there. Python sets `sys.dont_write_bytecode`; state goes to `~/.local/state` or `~/.config`. Two exceptions: `.git/` is ignored by the watcher, so `update check` may fetch; and `update apply` rewrites the folder on purpose, which is why it runs detached (`Quickshell.execDetached`) and reports through `~/.local/state/rog-deck/update.json`.
- **Integrated GPU mode is supergfxd's**, never a queued `dgpu_disable=1`: asusd writes queued GPU values at shutdown, nvidia_drm (from the initramfs) still holds the card then, and the write fails with EIO, leaving the laptop in Hybrid.
- **The power helper runs as root without a password**, so it takes no paths or free text: every value is checked against a fixed list, or against `LIMITS` for the board and the power source it reads itself, never below the minimums (some AMD laptops throttle until power-off under them). Change what it accepts only with `tests/test_power.py`, and bump its `VERSION` so the panel offers the update. The polkit rule must only ever name the root-owned copy, never a file in the plugin folder.
- **Battery saver and the CPU limit apply on a fresh status.** Triggers (charger, mode, wake, login) call `schedulePower`, which asks for a status and applies on the reply. Overdrive and Wi-Fi move only when the saver starts or stops holding, and for 15 s after; the CPU limit is rewritten after every trigger and on the recheck because the firmware loads each mode's limits on mode and charger changes and doesn't report them back. The firmware ignores limits unless the mode runs a custom fan curve: `power limit` switches Silent's on and records that in `~/.local/state/rog-deck/power.json`, and `power off` switches it off only if it did. Test a limit change with a sustained all-core load and the socket power in `gpu_metrics`, never by reading the sysfs value back.
- **Full speed only undoes its own change.** `speed on` (Balanced on the charger only, checked again in the backend) writes the `performance` energy preference and leaves a marker in `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/rog-deck/speed-held`; `speed off` hands the preference back to power-profiles-daemon's value only with that marker, so a preference the user set by hand in another mode stays. It never touches the governor: power-profiles-daemon owns that, and under the performance governor the kernel refuses preference writes, so `speed off` keeps the marker and waits for the next trigger while something holds that governor. It applies from `applyPower`, after power-profiles-daemon's own write on a mode or charger change has landed. A logout wipes the marker but not the preference. power-profiles-daemon never picks `performance` for Balanced, so the next `speed on` treats one in place as ROG Deck's and claims it; at worst the hand-back writes the daemon's own value.
- **Refresh rate and hyprmoncfg:** save the rate into the profile the daemon will match, never into one with screens that aren't connected. `hold_rate` keeps a one-screen "Laptop" profile for that. Name the panel by description in any `hl.monitor` rule, through `monitor_output`, never by connector: a rule for the connector inherits `disabled = true` from a leftover Omarchy clamshell flag and blanks the panel.
- **The bar's wheel steps in notches** (`Model.wheelSteps`, 120 units each). A touchpad sends dozens of small deltas per swipe; stepping on each ran Silent to Turbo in one swipe.
- **Optimistic picks clear only on a fresh read.** `Service.actionSeq` goes up as each action finishes; a status read remembers the seq it started at and only a read from after the last action clears `optimistic`. A read still running when an action lands is followed by another. Without it the mode pick flicked back for a poll.
- **The action queue keeps the newest of a kind** (`Model.actionKind`): idle-off/on, a CPU limit, a mode, a rate. One-offs (Full once, a fan preset or reset) always run. `power setup` runs in its own process, since its password prompt can wait minutes.
- **Warmth is held** (`Model.heatLevels`, kept on the status as `heat`): it climbs at once and drops only 3 °C under the step, so a reading on 85 °C doesn't flip the word, the colour, the hot mark and the icon every poll. Read it with `Model.heatOf`, never `heat(temp)` alone.
- **Throwing a curve away asks first.** Fan Reset, and a preset while that mode's custom curve is on, go through `Panel.pickFans` and its `ConfirmDialog` (`Model.fanConfirm`).
- **Quiet colours are tints**, `host.quiet(c, amount)` (the colour toward the background), never `Qt.darker`, which went black on dark themes and vanished on light ones. Check a change on a light theme too (flexoki-light).
- **QML changes need `omarchy restart shell`.** Hot reload can keep the old component cached.
- **Pure logic goes in `Model.js` or `components/Rooms.js`** (`.pragma library` files with no QML) so `node tests/*.test.js` can run it. Change it with a test.
- **Pixel art stays sharp.** `PixelIcon` and `PixelText` lay their cells from a whole device pixel, snapping their origin before each frame the window draws (`afterAnimating`): at 1.25 a 12-grid cell is exactly one device pixel, and from half a pixel off rounding doubled some strokes and dropped others, which turned the CPU and the GPU into blobs in the bar. `Rectangle`s with `antialiasing: false`, positions in whole cells of the icon's own grid (`PixelIcon.cell`), never `scale` or a turn other than a quarter (the gear's eighths while it turns, the gear button's and the cog icon's notch, are the one exception). Moving parts shift by whole 12-grid cells (`PixelIcon.step`) and turn by swapping frames, never by rotating. New motion goes in `Motion.js`, never a timer in the file that draws the icon.
- **Text is `MonoText`, sized by `step`.** Every text ROG Deck draws, and the kit's, is a `MonoText` (the kit's file, the same in all four plugins): give it `fontFamily: host.fontFamily` and `step: "small" | "body" | "title"`, and never set `font.family`, `font.pixelSize`, `font.bold`, `renderType`, `antialiasing` or `transform` on it, which it owns. Each step draws a font pixel as a whole number of device pixels (1, 2, 3 at 1 and 1.25; 2, 3, 4 at 1.5 and 2), so every stroke is sharp. Names and labels are `body` in `fg`; states, hints, captions, cells and the hover card are `small`, dimmed as `host.dim` (a tint toward the background); page titles are `title`. Hierarchy is step and colour, never bold: Monocraft's bold is off the grid. Its read-only `advance` (one character; Monocraft is monospaced) and `line` measure text without a FontMetrics. `tests/rooms.test.js` fails on a bare `Text` outside the panel kit. At 1.25 body is twice small, so check every view at 2 and at 1.25 with the longest labels the model makes: a state line beside a figure has about 20 characters in half a row.
- **Scale comes from the window.** Read `Window.window.devicePixelRatio`, never `Screen.devicePixelRatio`, which says 2 on the 1.25 desk monitors: `PixelIcon`, `PixelText`, `MonoText` and the bar's units all snap to it.
- Comments say why, in plain sentences. Match the surrounding code.

## Release

Bump `version` in `manifest.json` and the version badge in `README.md` together, run the tests, and describe the change for the person using it in the commit message.
